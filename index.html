  • Recycled Materials for Modern Technology

    From smartphones and laptops to smart devices and drones, modern technology depends on recycled materials. By recycling outdated electronics, ReMA’s members strengthen environmental sustainability, support the manufacturing supply chain, and meet rising demand for new technologies.

    Learn More About Electronics

  • International Trade
    and Market Access

    U.S. recyclers process more material than domestic manufacturers can use each year. ReMA members help power global manufacturing by keeping valuable recycled materials moving through international supply chains – supporting jobs, investment, and industry here at home.

    Access the latest trade information

  • Protecting U.S. Supply Chains

    Cargo theft and organized supply chain crime threaten recyclers, manufacturers, and the movement of valuable materials. ReMA supports stronger protections, including the Combatting Organized Retail Crime Act (CORCA), to help secure supply chains and protect businesses and workers.

    Read more about ReMA’s efforts

  • Recycling for Critical Mineral Security

    Recycled materials help supply the critical minerals needed for batteries, electronics, energy infrastructure, and advanced manufacturing. ReMA members recover and process these materials to strengthen domestic supply chains, support U.S. manufacturing, and help meet growing industrial demand.

    Learn more about critical minerals

  • Safety in Recycling Operations

    Safety is at the core of the recycled materials industry. ReMA members invest in training, equipment, and best practices to protect workers, reduce risk, and ensure responsible operations across every stage of the recycling supply chain.

    See how ReMA prioritizes safety

Who We Are

Meet ReMA

The Recycled Materials Association (ReMA) represents 1,700 recycling companies whose work supports a resilient supply chain and a more secure nation. Based in Washington, DC, we drive advocacy, workforce development and awareness of the critical role of recycled materials. Get to know our members and learn how the world is made with recycled materials.
